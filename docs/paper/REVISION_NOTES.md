# wavetopo manuscript — revision notes (2026-10-14)

`dolfinx_wave_control.tex` was restructured from a catalog of demonstrations into a
hypothesis-driven paper. `dolfinx_wave_control_v1_archive.tex` is the previous version, kept
verbatim.

## Structure
- **Question:** how much control over in-plane elastic waves does fiber orientation alone
  provide (fixed material and mass, tow-curvature limit); by what mechanism; what bounds it?
- **Hypothesis:** energy channelling. qP energy in CFRP skews up to 65° toward the fibers, and
  88% of wave normals carry their energy within 20° of the fiber, so a fiber field is a field of
  energy streamlines.
- **Predictions W1–W5**, each tested. There is a verdict table in the Discussion.
- Files: `dolfinx_wave_control.tex` (main), `ws_*.tex`, `wt_numbers.tex` and `wt_*_table.tex`
  (auto-generated by `examples/analysis/wt_paper_assets.py`), `wavetopo_extensions.tex`
  (companion notes: holes/joint routing, beam splitter, flat band, periodic multi-function,
  focus–stiffness, CST shield, bracket, valley-Hall).

## Corrections to claims in the previous draft
1. **The lens's 78× is mostly resonance.** 78× = 5.8× redirection × 13.4× growth of the whole
   interior field, and the design draws 4.1× more source power. At ±6% frequency the gain falls
   to 7–10×, while redirection stays ≥3.7× over ω ∈ [37.5, 60].
2. **The cloak's 36× is narrowband**: 7.5× and 15× at ±5%, and 1.9× at ω = 40, where the
   transformation-direction field does better (4.6×).
3. **"Cloak cannot meet 1/R_V"** came from the superseded soft-void study. The conforming cloak
   at ζ = 1/R_V gives 9.6×.
4. **Sign convention.** The solver uses e^{+iωt} (K(1+iη) − ω²M + iωC). The paper stated
   e^{−iωt} with a −iωc term, which would mean negative damping. Fixed.
5. **Parameter table.** The cloak column listed a superseded soft-void configuration (ω = 36,
   6×4). Replaced with the driver's actual values (ω = 50, 8×5, R_C = 1.7, conforming mesh).
6. The lens curvature study (47×→12×) used a coarser 80-element mesh than the main lens (78×).
   It was replaced by a consistent front on the 130-element mesh.
7. **Mesh claim.** The claim that "every hole-conforming result is scalar" was false and has
   been removed.
